In this chapter, new arithmetic operations of triangular intuitionistic fuzzy
numbers are proposed. Also, a new method for comparing two triangular
intuitionistic fuzzy numbers is proposed. Finally, a new method (named as Mehar
method-III) is proposed to find more-for-less solutions of symmetric intuitionistic
fuzzy transportation problems with mixed constraints.
