Chapter 7  Text mining

7.1  Introduction

Because of the extent of digitization in the transportation sector
and the advancements in social media usage, large amounts of
textual data are generated in the form of reports, documents, and
social media posts. Analyzing this textual data can generate easily
understandable knowledge. Text mining helps understand the
structure and meaning of human language by analyzing the words
in the text. It converts unstructured text in documents, social media
posts, and databases into structured data suitable for data analysis.
Text mining tasks include data extraction from the sources,
preprocessing the text into tokens, cleaning the text, creating a bag
of words, and then further performing text mining on the tokens by
finding word frequencies, text visualization, topic modeling,
sentiment analysis, etc. Text mining can either be conducted as a
standalone process, or it can be used as a preprocessing step for
performing other data analysis with the summarized data obtained
through text mining.

  Text mining in transportation can be applied to roadways,
railways, and other sectors for accident and crash analysis, traffic
and mobility analysis, transportation construction and
infrastructure, driver behavior analysis, smart city planning, etc. In
this chapter, we apply different text mining techniques to extract
useful information from the textual data using R software.

as .txt files, .csv files, .pdf files, or through webscraping. We will

same words that occur in upper and lower case equally. Stemming
is another form of normalization where words are reduced to their
stem. By applying stemming, words with different suffixes are

7.2.5  Topic modeling and clustering

Topic modeling is a data mining approach that is used to identify
terms that go together in documents. Topic modeling methods help
identify the topics within a large corpus without human
intervention. It results in fast information retrieval since documents
do not need to be read to identify the important topics within them.
Popular topic modeling techniques are: hierarchical clustering, K-
means clustering, and latent dirichlet algorithm (LDA). While all the
methods identify topics, they differ in speed and accuracy.

7.2.5.1  Hierarchical clustering

Clustering groups words such that words within a group are similar
to each other and dissimilar to the other groups. In hierarchical
clustering, the proximate terms are iteratively grouped creating a
tree diagram called a “dendrogram.” Hierarchical clustering is a
good starting point for clustering as it does not require prior
knowledge of the number of clusters. We will use the preprocessed
corpus: the term document matrix “Pdfcorpus.tdm” created in
Section 7.2.2 of this chapter to apply hierarchical clustering and
identify the important topics within the pdf document. A view of the
term document matrix after removing the sparse terms can be seen
