by the zero-noise limit of the SOT, the minimizer of which is an h-path process. We
also show that the zero-noise limit of the Duality Theorem for the SOT gives the
Duality Theorem for the OT. The purpose of such research is that we would like
to study the OT in the framework of the SOT as a problem of the principle of least
action for particle systems.

2.3.1 Monge’s Problem by the Zero-Noise Limit of SOT

ness of the minimizer of Monge’s problem with a quadratic cost, independently of
Kantorovich’s approach (see [108]). In this sense, the SOT can be also considered
the OT with a random external force.
