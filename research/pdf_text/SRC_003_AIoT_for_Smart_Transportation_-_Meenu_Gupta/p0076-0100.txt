3.7.1 Emission reduction solutions

AIoT helps reduce emissions by enhancing traffic flow and reducing congestion.
Below are several such traffic reduction options:

l Adaptive traffic lights: Picture a red-to-review algorithm that can change based

  no reason, and instead the light will be green.

l Eco-driving better driving: Giving drivers feedback on spots to increase their
  driving behaviour efficiency and encouraging ‘green’ eco-driving, such as
  maintaining an optimum speed while not accelerating and braking as required.

l AI route selection: To reduce delivery route travel time and improve fuel
  efficiency, people use AI to optimise trucking routes by analysing distances
  and managing goods distribution.

l Load optimisation; By ensuring that a truck is fully loaded and load distribu-
  tion is rational, they cover the maximum possible part in one trip.

  return results in a load balance and also offers maximum use of renewable
