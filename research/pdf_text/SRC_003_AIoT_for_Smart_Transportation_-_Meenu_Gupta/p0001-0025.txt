  Preface

The convergence of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and IoT (Internet of Things) offers
unparalleled opportunities in the domain of transportation, to redefine how mobility
is perceived, managed, and experienced. IoT deals with devices interacting through
the internet and is made up of interconnected objects with built-in sensors which
has the potential to generate or collect a vast amount of data, AI enables these
devices to learn from their data and experience to produce next-generation intelli-
gent transportation systems.

  The book, AIoT for Smart Transportation: Transforming the Future of Mobility,
explores the dynamic intersection of AIoT technologies and their pivotal role in
shaping the future of transportation systems. The books address the critical challenges
posed by urbanisation, and the demand for efficient, safe, and sustainable mobility
solutions. This book provides a comprehensive analysis of state-of-the-art technolo-
gies, methodologies, and applications and a roadmap for researchers, practitioners,
and policymakers to navigate the evolving landscape of smart transportation.

  This book is structured and comprises of contributions from leading experts in
academia and industry to provide both theoretical insights and practical perspec-
tives offering a multidimensional view of how AIoT technologies are deployed to
tackle real-world transportation challenges. The book highlights innovative appli-
cations and emerging trends that are set to redefine mobility paradigms and con-
tains theoretical and practical knowledge of the most recent state-of-the-art AIoT
for Smart Transportation. The book applies various AI techniques as well as pro-
mising future research attempts in the transportation domain. The book consists of
15 quality chapters on the below mentioned topics. It is a result of the handwork of
outstanding researchers around the globe.

  The target audience for this book includes academics, researchers, engineers,
urban planners, and policymakers who are keen on understanding and leveraging
the potential of AIoT in transportation. By addressing critical issues such as data
security, ethical considerations, and interoperability, the book ensures a holistic
exploration of the subject.

  It is our hope that this book serves as both a comprehensive guide and an
inspiration for stakeholders committed to building a smarter, more connected
future. We extend our deepest gratitude to the contributors, reviewers, and pub-
lishers whose efforts have brought this vision to life. Together, let us embark on
this journey to unlock the transformative potential of AIoT for smart transportation.
xviii AIoT for smart transportation

Overview of the chapter content

Chapter 1 explores the transformative potential of integrating AI and IoT in modern
transportation systems. It highlights how AIoT-driven solutions address pressing
challenges. The evolution of smart transportation and key components, the benefits,
challenges, and real-world applications of AIoT are discussed.

  Chapter 2 Introduces how the AIoT is revolutionising contemporary trans-
portation. The overview of the AIoT, real-world examples, and its potential for the
future are among the main points. The chapter also examines the advantages and
difficulties of AIoT, highlighting its role in building more intelligent and net-
worked transportation systems.

  Chapter 3 discusses the potential of AIoT to improve urban mobility and
modify Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS) to satisfy the changing needs of
future cities. It describes how ITS employs computers, electronics, and commu-
nication technologies to increase traffic efficiency through automated systems.

  Chapter 4 examines how traffic control, public transit, and road safety via
vehicle-to-vehicle communication, route optimisation and sustainability are
achieved. Case studies of intelligent traffic systems in various cities are covered.
Future trends and problems are examined.

  Introduction to AIoT in smart transportation

  Anjuli Goel1 and Chander Prabha1

Abstract

As technology develops, our society acquires increasingly complex devices that facil-
itate the more effective and efficient completion of daily chores. The Internet of Things
(IoT) is one of the biggest technological advances of our time; it connects a multitude
of smart devices and makes data sharing and interaction simple. Artificial Intelligence
is another breakthrough technology that contributes to data analysis and decision-
making. Thanks to new technologies such as artificial intelligence and the IoT, it has
become possible to create smart transportation systems capable of enhancing drivers’
safety, better flow of traffic, and decreasing the cost of transportation. Smart transport
systems have emerged as a possible approach to increasing transport efficiency, safety,
and sustainability. This chapter gives a comprehensive view of smart transportation.
The various stages that have taken place in the evolution of smart transportation are
discussed, from no technology used to autonomous transport. Smart transportation
consists of three components – smart vehicles, smart parking, and smart roads. The
shortcomings in traditional transportation are given to better understand the importance
of smart transportation. The various benefits and emerging trends of Artificial
Intelligence of Things (AIoT) in smart transportation are explained. The technologies
that help in the deployment of AIoT in smart transportation are highlighted. Lastly, the
various challenges in implementing smart transportation are focused on.

  Keywords: Intelligent; Smart; Transport; Environmental; Safety; Autono-
  mous; Machine learning

1.1 Introduction

Smart transportation makes commuting around a city easier, less expensive, and
safer by using new and developing technology. In the words of the US Department
of Transportation, ‘Intelligent transportation systems use a set of various technol-
ogies to control, assess, and administer the transportation systems to improve their
efficiency and safety’ [1]. Smart transportation systems may be utilised to enhance

traffic lights in cities. Rather than depending on traditional pre-timed traffic lights,
these signals may now use previous data and artificial intelligence (AI) to assess
traffic movements in real time and make necessary modifications. Smart transport
eliminates the need to sit at a traffic signal for minutes even when traffic is clear
[2]. Smart Transportation Systems mean the integration of management practices,
computer and electronics, communication technologies, and traveller information
to enhance the effectiveness and safety of surface transportation. To improve the
safety and capacity of the road system, these systems integrate the interactions of
automobiles, drivers, passengers, road operators, and management with the envir-

existing transport infrastructure and enhance traffic management by utilising a
range of wireline and wireless communication-based information and electronics
technologies. It improves driving conditions, road network capacity, safety, and
efficiency. It also reduces transportation risks, eases traffic congestion, boosts
transportation effectiveness, and lessens pollution [3].
