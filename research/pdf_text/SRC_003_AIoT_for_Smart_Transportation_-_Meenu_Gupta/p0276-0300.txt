13.3.1 Pre-processing of traffic camera footage

To prepare traffic camera footage for effective analysis by an AIoT model, several
pre-processing steps are essential. Noise reduction techniques, such as Gaussian
blurring or median filtering, can be applied to remove noise and enhance image
quality. Image enhancement methods, like histogram equalization or gamma cor-
rection, can improve brightness, making it easier for the model to recognize sig-
nificant features. Image resizing is another important step to ensure consistent input
to the AI model. Images can be resized to a standard size using techniques like
bilinear or bicubic interpolation, which help maintain image quality. Data stan-

augmentation can improve model performance by generating additional training
data through random transformations like rotation, scaling, shearing, and flipping.

lighting scenarios. By effectively pre-processing traffic camera footage, we can
improve the accuracy and robustness of the AIoT model for emergency vehicle
