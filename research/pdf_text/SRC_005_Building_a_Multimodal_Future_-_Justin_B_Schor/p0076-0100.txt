  establish a TDM program in the development conditions for the
  development.

  Post–site plan approval: No TDM is required post–site plan
  approval.

  Preconstruction: Typically, a TDM plan must be completed and
  submitted to the county as a prerequisite for obtaining the site
  building permit.

  Preoccupancy: Typically, the developer must show that it has begun
  implementing the TDM program. It must also show that it has set
  up and funded the mechanisms for TDM implementation and
  enforcement. For projects in Tysons Corner, developers must submit
  a TDM work plan that outlines the individual steps required for
  implementation of strategies, includes a communications plan for
  the program, contains monitoring and evaluation logistics, and
  estimates an associated startup and ongoing budget to implement
  the TDM program.

  Postoccupancy: TDM marketing is implemented in addition to
  ongoing monitoring and evaluation.
